How to play

Tap a number, an operation, then another number. The result becomes a new tile. Reach the target to solve automatically. Undo restores the previous numbers.

Score carries between rounds. Lose a heart when time runs out. Every 3 solved rounds earns +1 heart (up to 6).

Easy: 1–2 operations. Medium: 3. Hard: 4–5. Faster solutions earn more points.
